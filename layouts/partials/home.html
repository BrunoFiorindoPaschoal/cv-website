About

I am an M.Sc. student in Mechatronics, Robotics and Biomechanical Engineering at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), after completing my B.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering at the Polytechnic School of the University of São Paulo (USP) through the TUM–USP Double Degree program.

I am interested in Mathematical Optimization, Machine Learning, Responsible AI, Robotics and Scientific Computing.

I currently work as a Student Research Assistant at the TUM Laboratory for Product Development and Lightweight Design (LPL), supervised by Prof. Markus Zimmermann, building surrogate models and Solution Space methods for Multidisciplinary Design Optimization.

Previously, I was an undergraduate research scholar at the MSOL (Multiphysics Systems Optimization Laboratory) at USP, funded by FAPESP, working on topology optimization of switched reluctance motors and on hydraulic and mechanical neural networks.
