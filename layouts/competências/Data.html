Machine Learning

I have always had a strong interest in the field of machine learning. I have completed several courses in this area, including the university-level course “Machine Learning and Pattern Recognition” (PMR3508) at the Polytechnic School of the University of São Paulo, taught by Prof. Dr. Fabio Gagliardi Cozman. The course provided an in-depth understanding of the numerical theory behind machine learning and included the implementation of various classification algorithms.

In parallel, I have explored the physical implementation of learning systems through the development of mechanical and hydraulic neural networks — physical systems inspired by artificial neural networks, capable of performing tasks such as classification, control, and adaptation using mechanical or fluidic interactions instead of digital computation.

These devices were optimized using topology optimization and simulated using the finite element method. My research includes proposals for real-world applications in smart structures and soft robotics, supported by FAPESP and supervised by Prof. Dr. Emílio Carlos Nelli Silva.
